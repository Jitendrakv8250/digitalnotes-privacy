Privacy Policy for DigitalNotes

Effective date: 18 September 2026
App: DigitalNotes (Android)
Package: com.jv.digitalnotes
Developer: Jitendra

DigitalNotes (“the App”) is designed to work entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account.
  • The App does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or sale.
  • The App does not send your notes to our servers. We do not operate a backend for this App.
  • Notes, photos, and PDFs stay in the App’s private storage on your phone unless you choose to share them.

2. Information the App stores on your device

When you use DigitalNotes, the following may be stored locally on your device only:

  • Note titles and chapter names you enter
  • Images you capture with the camera or select from your gallery
  • PDF files generated from those images
  • App metadata needed to list and open your notes (for example, note IDs and timestamps)

This content is stored in the App’s private application storage. Cloud backup of App data is disabled by the App.

3. Permissions

The App may ask for:

  • Camera — to scan or photograph pages for your notes
  • Photos / media (images) — to let you pick existing images from your gallery

These permissions are used only to create and manage notes on your device. The App does not use them to upload content in the background.

4. Network and third parties

The App is built to run offline and does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs that collect data for us.

If you use the Share / Export PDF feature, Android’s system share sheet opens. You choose which app receives the PDF (for example Gmail, WhatsApp, or Telegram). Those apps are operated by their own providers and have their own privacy policies. DigitalNotes only prepares the PDF on your device and hands it to the app you select.

5. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Anyone can use the App for on-device note scanning; we do not create profiles or track users.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your notes remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App (which removes the App’s private data). Because data is not uploaded to us, we cannot recover notes after deletion or uninstall.

7. Security

Notes are stored in Android app-private storage. The App does not transmit note content over the network as part of normal use. You are responsible for the physical security of your device and for any content you choose to share through other apps.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s behavior changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when that happens. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
